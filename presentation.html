PROBLEM ANALYSIS

The University Commerce Dilemma

Why unstructured public social platforms and general classified sites fail university campuses.

⚠️ 1. Unstructured Social Media Feeds
Facebook & WhatsApp groups bury important book and equipment requests under hundreds of irrelevant posts within minutes.
⚠️ 2. Rampant Advance Payment Scams
Anonymous online sellers exploit bKash/Nagad advance transfers with zero identity verification and no dispute recourse.
⚠️ 3. Heavy Academic Financial Burden
Students pay 3x–5x higher costs purchasing brand-new textbooks, calculators, and lab tools each semester.
⚠️ 4. Zero Campus Identity Verification
Public classified platforms allow unauthorized outsiders onto campus premises without institutional accountability.
Live Wanted Requests: Demonstrating the high student demand for missing campus supplies
VALUE PROPOSITION

The UniMart AI Solution

A grounded, closed-loop student marketplace with 4 distinct commerce modes.

🔐 Walled-Garden Institutional Auth
Strictly restricted to verified @bup.edu.bd student credentials. Guaranteed accountability and elimination of anonymous fraudsters.
🔄 4 Versatile Commerce Modes
Direct Sell (discounted price), Barter Exchange (item-for-item), 0 BDT Free Giveaways (donations), and Student Wanted Requests.
🤝 Zero-Scam Handover Protocol
Zero online pre-payment risk. Mandatory bilateral digital confirmation when students meet at verified campus locations.
🤖 Grounded Multilingual AI Assistant
Contextual NLP assistant handling English, Bangla, and Banglish queries with automatic out-of-stock Wanted routing.
Institutional Verification: Student ID, ICT Department & Verified University Badge

CORE FUNCTIONALITY

Four Inclusive Commerce Modes

Addressing the diverse financial capabilities and academic needs of students across all terms.

1. Direct Sale + Make Offer
Fixed-price purchases or bilateral bargaining with counter-offers, expiration times, and automated status reservation.
2. Barter Exchange (Item Swapping)
Cashless student trade. Students offer their active listings in exchange for textbooks, drafting kits, or electronics.
3. 0 BDT Free Giveaways
Philanthropic donation of lecture notes, lab manuals, and guides. Recipients provide a short academic reason.
4. Student Wanted Requests
Inverted demand board: buyers post what they need, and peer sellers respond with customized offers.
Active Student Listings Management (Direct Sale, Edit & Availability Controls)

SECURITY & VERIFICATION

Zero-Scam Protocol & Verified Chat

Eliminating fraud through cryptographic security and closed-loop student messaging.

🤝 2-Step Dual Confirmation Handover
Advance transfers forbidden. Students meet at well-lit campus landmarks (Library, Cafeteria). Both parties tap 'Confirm Handover' to conclude deals.
💬 Closed-Loop Real-Time Messaging
Direct in-app messaging between authenticated BUP students. Eliminates external stalking or private phone number exposure.
🔑 Cryptographic Authentication
Passwords protected with BCrypt hashing with salt rounds. Sessions managed via signed stateless JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
🛡️ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Strict security boundary separating Student permissions from Administrative Moderation and abuse audit logs.
Closed-Loop In-App Messaging between Verified Campus Peers
