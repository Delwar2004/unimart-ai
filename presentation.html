Slide 02 // Problem Statement Current Campus Challenges

The University Commerce Dilemma

Why Traditional Facebook Groups & General Classifieds Fail University Students

⚠️ Fragmented & Lost Posts
Trade currently happens across informal Facebook groups and Messenger chats. Items get buried within minutes beneath unrelated posts, lacking categories and search.
🚨 Advance Payment Scams
Unverified sellers often demand advance bKash/Nagad payments and disappear. Without identity accountability, students have zero legal or institutional recourse.
💸 Heavy Financial Burden
Brand-new engineering books, calculators, and lab tools cost thousands of BDT every semester, while graduating seniors throw away reusable assets due to lack of a structured channel.

Slide 05 // Functionality Comprehensive Marketplace Features

Four Inclusive Commerce Modes

Ensuring Academic Equipment is Accessible to Every Student Regardless of Budget

1. Peer-to-Peer Selling with "Make Offer"
Fixed price checkout or dynamic student bargaining with counter-offers, instant expiration timers, and transaction tracking.
2. Barter Exchange (Item Swapping)
100% cashless trade. A 1st-year student can swap their past semester book directly for a 2nd-year drawing board.
3. 0 BDT Free Giveaways (Senior Donations)
Philanthropic shelf where seniors pass down course notes, question banks, and lab coats to juniors at zero cost.
4. Crowdsourced Wanted Requests
Reverses marketplace mechanics: students post out-of-stock demands, and campus peers respond with matching offers.

Slide 07 // Trust & Security Zero-Scam Protocol

2-Step Handover Protocol & Security

Eliminating Online Payment Fraud Through Verified Physical Dual Confirmation

1. No Advance Online Payments
Completely removes the risk of courier fraud or fake payment receipts. All transactions are settled in-person on campus.
2. Mandatory In-Person Inspection
Students meet at pre-designated campus hotspots (Central Library, BUP Cafeteria, Campus Plaza) to physically inspect item condition.
3. Bilateral Digital Confirmation
Both Buyer and Seller must press 'Confirm Handover' in their respective dashboards to finalize the transaction status.
4. Cryptographic Authentication
BCrypt salted password hashing, stateless JWT session tokens, and strict Role-Based Access Control (Student vs Admin Moderation).
